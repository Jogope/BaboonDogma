Core Gameplay

Forge Your Dynasty in the Arena

I

Ludus Management

Build barracks, armories, and infirmaries on an isometric grid. Balance food rations, physician contracts, and trainer payrolls.

II

Stat-Driven Tactical Combat

Assign stances, flanking orders, and tactical plans. AI simulates combat in real time driven by warrior attributes, conditioning, and injuries.

III

Political Intrigues

Navigate the Senate, the priesthood, and the criminal underworld. Accept bribes, rig matchings, or face retribution for defying the patricians.

IV

Procedural Gladiators

Every warrior features procedural traits, dynamic morale, permanent scars, and individual goals. No two rosters or seasons are ever the same.

V

Tournaments & Exhibitions

Register champions in lethal underground fight nights, state funeral duels, and grand Colosseum games for immense purses and glory.

VI

Narrative Dilemmas

Encounter tavern contracts, guild bribes, and shady propositions. Every choice has a lasting price in gold, infamy, or gladiator blood.
