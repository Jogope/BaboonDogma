Game Pillars

FORGED IN BLOOD & GREED

You are not the gladiator dying in the dust. You are the lanista orchestrating their lives, wealth, and destiny.

Isometric Ludus Management

Design and expand your training barracks on a detailed isometric grid. Balance your daily denarii expenses, hire veteran doctores, build surgical infirmaries, and secure supply lines of grain and wine to keep rebellion at bay.

Infrastructure & Logistics 01

Stat-Driven Tactical Combat

No frantic button mashing. Battles are resolved in real-time, auto-simulated encounters governed entirely by your gladiators' conditioning, weapon proficiencies, stamina reserves, and specific tactical instructions.

Rigorous Simulation 02

Senate & Underworld Politics

The real fight happens off the sands. Navigate four competing political factions, bribe corrupt magistrates, dodge rival assassinations, and strike clandestine pacts with criminal rings for match-fixing and illicit gold.

Intrigue & Factions 03

Procedural Champions

Every recruit brings unique traits, psychological quirks, and physical limitations. Manage permanent battle scars, amputations, infection risks, and morale breakdowns — or grant freedom to legends who earn their wooden rudis.
